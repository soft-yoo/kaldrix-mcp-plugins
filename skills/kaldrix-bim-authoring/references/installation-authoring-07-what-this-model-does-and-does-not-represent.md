## What this model does and does not represent

- Components are the kinds in the runtime point resource. Do not invent kinds for pumps, detailed valves, boilers' internal ports or cable accessories. Free labels/notes describe supported components; they add no physical component or quantity rule.
- Fittings derive from circuit geometry; no fitting placement tool. One circuit = one conduit spec: separate circuits for different pipe/cable sizes, keeping supported source/target relationships.
- Room underfloor heating: `preview_underfloor_heating` → `create_underfloor_heating` from UFH_MANIFOLD at the preview modelVersion. Orthogonal rooms, SPIRAL/MEANDER, exclusions and multiple loops are supported; unsupported geometry fails explicitly. Corners and approaches are schematic (no physical bending/separation); geometry and quantity takeoff only, not thermal or hydraulic sizing. Never model these loops as paired HEATING_SUPPLY_RETURN.
- Plain routes (drawn in the app, listed in `get_floor_detail`) are separate from points/circuits and have no MCP editing tools; drawing over a point does not connect it. Use nodeEdit for a connected manual circuit. Exploding a circuit loses parametric behavior and needs explicit deletion authorization.
- Routing, warnings and quantities are supported; never claim pressure-loss, thermal-load, short-circuit, full collision-clearance or regulatory certification calculations these tools do not return.

Finally read circuit layouts and riser warnings, validate the floor, then `price_estimate_from_model` (stage INSTALLATIONS) and `review_estimate_against_model`. CIRCUIT_LENGTH already includes backend reserve, paired heating and parallel runs; never multiply again. Do not price both a circuit and its duplicated plain route. Report actual connected IDs, unresolved warnings and unsupported requested components explicitly.

## What this model does and does not represent

- Individual components are the kinds in the runtime point resource. Do not invent kinds for pumps, detailed valves, boilers' internal ports or cable accessories. Free labels/notes describe existing supported components; they do not add a new physical component or quantity rule.
- Fittings are derived from circuit geometry; there is no independent fitting placement tool. One circuit has one conduit spec: use separate circuits for different pipe/cable sizes, and retain supported source/target relationships.
- Room underfloor heating uses `preview_underfloor_heating` and `create_underfloor_heating`, sourced from UFH_MANIFOLD. Use the preview modelVersion when applying. Orthogonal rooms, SPIRAL/MEANDER, exclusions and multiple loops are supported; unsupported geometry fails explicitly. Corners and approaches are schematic and do not describe physical pipe bending or separation. This is geometry and quantity takeoff, not thermal or hydraulic sizing. Do not model these loops as paired HEATING_SUPPLY_RETURN.
- Plain routes (drawn in the app, listed in `get_floor_detail`) are separate from points/circuits and have no MCP editing tools; drawing over a point does not connect it. Use nodeEdit for a connected manual circuit. Exploding a circuit loses parametric circuit behavior and needs explicit deletion authorization.
- Routing, warnings and quantities are supported; do not claim pressure-loss, thermal-load, short-circuit, full collision-clearance or regulatory certification calculations that these tools do not return.

Finally read circuit layouts and riser warnings, validate the floor, then use `price_estimate_from_model` (stage INSTALLATIONS) and `review_estimate_against_model`. CIRCUIT_LENGTH includes backend reserve, paired heating and parallel runs; never multiply these again. Avoid pricing both a circuit and its duplicated plain route. Report actual connected IDs, unresolved warnings and unsupported requested components explicitly.

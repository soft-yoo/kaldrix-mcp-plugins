## Renovation without a drawing

Not every project needs a model. For a renovation or pricing without a drawing, create the project with
`workType=RENOVATION` (or switch with `update_project`): the MODEL step becomes optional and the workflow goes from
the address straight to the estimate with manual quantities. The rest of this guide applies only when a drawing is
wanted. After the offer both work types follow the section "From the accepted offer to the keys" of
`kaldrix://mcp/agent-workflow`.

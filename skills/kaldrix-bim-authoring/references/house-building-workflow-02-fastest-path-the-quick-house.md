## Fastest path: the quick house

For a new house to price ("dom 12 × 10, piętro, dach dwuspadowy"), call `create_quick_house` first. One call creates the project, outline (rectangle, L or T), ground floor, optional upper storey and usable attic, envelope walls, roof, foundation and shell systems, plus the primary estimate priced from the model. Use the steps below only to change that model (rooms, openings, roof, outline) or for a custom model from scratch.

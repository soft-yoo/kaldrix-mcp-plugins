## Permissions: you act as the person

Each Kaldrix MCP connection acts for one person in one company chosen on the consent screen (with several eligible companies, the person selects one first). Switching companies in the app does not change an existing connector's company; reconnect to choose another. There is no separate "AI agent" permission: an active person in a company with the AI module and AI connections enabled may connect an agent, which gets only supported connector operations within that person's access, narrowed by consent and enabled modules. Some app operations never reach MCP.

- **Where permissions come from.** Each person has one company access template. Templates belong to the company: they start as the four Kaldrix patterns (Administrator, Site manager, Worker, Guest) and the company may edit, add or delete them. A template grants areas (projects, estimates, invoices, documents, team …) at a level: view (`<area>.read`), changes (`<area>.write`) or full access (`<area>.delete`). The fixed "No access" ("Brak dostępu") template blocks every site, including sites earlier assigned or shared; people land there when their template is deleted. Baseline account scopes may remain (the tool list need not be empty) but grant no site data. The owner always has every permission; for other templates, site access (all or selected sites) further limits project data.
- **Template names are not authorization.** They are editable labels, not fixed roles. Read actual person scopes, project visibility and connector consent. Even an owner's connector cannot bypass missing consent, an excluded module or an unavailable tool.
- **What this connection gets.** The person's current permissions narrowed to what they approved on consent, re-checked on every call: a removed permission stops working immediately; a new one needs a reconnect approving the new scope.
- **Workspace and account lifecycle is app-only.** MCP cannot create workspaces, create or delete user accounts, invite people, create or remove memberships, or delete the connected workspace — use the Kaldrix app. Existing tools read people or update business records only within declared scopes and the person's live access. A crew record without a login is a business record, not a Kaldrix account. An owner subscription covering several workspaces never grants access across them or changes an existing OAuth connection's workspace. Never use generic GraphQL, REST or another tool as a bypass.
- **Never available to connectors**, whatever the person may do in the app: account and sign-in security, team and permission management, company settings, subscription and payments, the KSeF connection. Some removals (e.g. deleting a whole project) exist only in the app.
- **When a call is refused** (`MISSING_SCOPE` with `requiredScope`, `FORBIDDEN`, `WORKSPACE_ACCESS_DENIED`, a `*_PERMISSION_REQUIRED` code or a site not shared with the person): do not retry, do not look for another tool doing the same, never ask for passwords or tokens. Tell the user plainly which area or site is missing: a team manager can change their template in Team → Permissions (or share the site); if they already have the permission in the app, reconnect and approve that scope.
- **Project versions.** Listing and previewing need `history.read`, saving `history.write` (both with `projects.read`), restoring `history.restore` (with `projects.write`; restoring estimates also `estimates.write`). Consent lists `history.restore` with removals, unchecked by default.
- **Activity log.** Every tool call is recorded in AI → AI activity history ("Historia pracy AI"). Each person sees their own agents' actions; people with the Team permission (`members.read`) see the whole company.

Saved-data updates to contracts, warranties, crew assignments, time entries and tasks need write and delete scopes of their area plus `confirm=true` after the user reviews the change. Invoice due-date and stage updates need `invoices.write` and `invoices.delete`; site-location updates `project-settings.write` and `project-settings.delete`. A write-only connection must be re-authorized for these scopes within the person's current permissions. First assignments through these flagged tools need the same confirmation as replacements. `confirm=true` is a Boolean sent by the agent, not proof of a separate human click: get the user's actual decision first; never infer approval from the flag, an error or a retry.

## From the accepted offer to the keys

Backend-computed phases: PREPARATION (address, customer, model, estimate, offer) → AGREEMENT (contract, start) → REALIZATION (construction stages, construction log, progress, invoices) → HANDOVER (final acceptance) → CLOSED. `get_project_workflow` returns the phase, the phase bar (`phases`) and the next step (`nextStepId`, e.g. CONTRACT, START, SCHEDULE, HANDOVER, CLOSE). Never infer a phase, stage state, amount or instalment state. A step of a company module that is off disappears and never blocks the rest (its tool returns `MODULE_DISABLED`).

Editing a protocol, punch item or warranty defect (`update_acceptance_protocol`, `update_punch_item`, `update_warranty_defect`) needs `realization.write`, `realization.delete` and `confirm=true` after user approval; creation is separate.

1. Offer accepted: the client accepts the sent offer under the link (`get_estimate_offer_workflow.latestOffer`). The accepted estimate becomes primary (if the primary one is already approved, it is a second scope with its own contract); its prices and contract quantities freeze, only executed quantities change (`update_estimate_sheet` with `actualQuantity`).
2. Contracts (OFFERS module, optional): one per accepted offer — several per project for different works and clients, each with own annexes.
   - `list_project_contracts`: totals, project deadline (the latest), `preparableEstimates` (accepted offers without a contract).
   - `create_project_contract` (projectId, optional estimateId) drafts from that offer: parties (the offer's client), scope, remuneration, schedule dates, proposed instalments — 20% advance at signing, 70% after the stages from estimate sections, 10% after the final handover.
   - Adjust: `update_project_contract`, `set_project_contract_tranches` (`useProposal=true` restores the proposal). Later tools take `contractId`; `get_project_contract` reads one. `sendBlockedBy` must be empty before sending.
   - Then `send_project_contract` (confirm=true; message on behalf of the company; client accepts online) or, after a paper signature, `mark_project_contract_signed` (`contracts.write`, confirm=true, cannot be undone). `return_project_contract_to_draft` (confirm=true; link stops working) returns a sent contract for corrections; `cancel_project_contract` cancels it (`contracts.delete`, confirm=true).
   - Formalities (Polish Construction Law checklist, not legal advice): `get_project_formalities` lists what to settle before, during and after the works, with due dates and warnings (e.g. notify building control (PINB) of the intended start before works start; completion notice before moving in). `update_project_formality` records one formality's state (`realization.write`). The most important missing one appears in `get_project_workflow.notices`; it never blocks a step.
3. Start: `get_project_realization` shows start options and the recommended stage source. `start_project_realization` records the start and creates stages: FROM_ESTIMATE (one per estimate section, so instalments after a section find their stage), FROM_PROJECT_PARTS, KEEP_EXISTING or NONE; the project moves to REALIZATION. Works may start without an accepted offer (oral agreement, own build); the workflow suggests the offer first.
4. Running the works:
   - `get_project_schedule` (SCHEDULE module): stages with dates, state, progress, forecast, delay. Estimate-section stages take progress from executed quantities of the accepted estimate; others use `set_schedule_stage_progress` (manual value, or `fromEstimate=true` to go back).
   - Log entries belong to a stage: `list_construction_log_entries` lists stages and today's (`defaultStageId`) and filters by stage; send that `stageId` to `add_construction_log_entry`.
   - Instalments: `record_contract_tranche_payment` (confirm=true only after the user confirms the payment); undo a wrong one with `clear_contract_tranche_payment` (confirm=true).
   - Scope change during works (additional, replacement or omitted works, later completion) = annex to the concluded contract, never an edit of the accepted estimate. `list_contract_annexes` (state, amounts, `defaultVatRate`); `get_contract_annex_sources` (contract positions with quantity in force and agreed price, sections, estimate price list; `includeModelProposal=true` compares the current model); `create_contract_annex` (ADDITIONAL / OMITTED lines: `CONTRACT_POSITION` with `positionKey` at contract price, `PRICE_LIST` with `recipeId` priced by the estimate engine, or `MANUAL` with unit prices in minor units; optional new schedule stage, `deadlineShiftDays`, payment mode, `idempotencyKey`) or, after model changes, `create_contract_annex_from_model` (not an approved-estimate edit). Then `send_contract_annex` (confirm=true; client accepts or rejects on the investor site page or annex link) or, after a paper signature, `mark_contract_annex_signed` (`contracts.write`, confirm=true). An agreed annex changes remuneration, instalments, completion date, settlement stage plan and material list (annex layer: `get_estimate_annex_changes`); a rejected one changes nothing.
   - Stage materials (ESTIMATES module): `get_project_material_list` gives primary-estimate materials (quantity to order, waste, packages — same rule as `get_estimate_purchase_resources`) by stage, with the nearest unfinished stage (`nextGroupKey`, filter `nextStageOnly=true`). After buying or delivery: `set_material_list_items_state` (BOUGHT, DELIVERED, TO_BUY). State survives estimate changes; a quantity changed after purchase shows `quantityChanged`. PDF and wholesaler e-mail stay in the app.
   - Crews and hours: crews and assignments need SCHEDULE; logging/reading hours needs TIME_TRACKING (works without SCHEDULE; entries then have no stage). `list_crews`: crews (people, trades, subcontractor account). `assign_crew_to_stage` puts a crew or one person on a stage (dates follow the stage unless given) and returns conflicts (same crew/person on another site the same days) as warnings; a labour-hour stage takes its duration from assigned headcount (`scheduleChanged` — reread the schedule). `get_project_crew_assignments` lists site assignments. `add_time_entry` logs hours (yourself, a person, a crew member or a whole crew with headcount); `get_project_time_sheet` totals per stage and person (hours only, no costs).
5. Stage acceptance: `create_acceptance_protocol` (kind STAGE, `stageId`); fill via `update_acceptance_protocol` (participants, notes, result); defects via `create_punch_item` (required for ACCEPTED_WITH_DEFECTS); check `signBlockedBy` in `get_acceptance_protocol`; then `sign_acceptance_protocol` (`realization.write`, confirm=true after the person on site agrees). A signed STAGE protocol with ACCEPTED or ACCEPTED_WITH_DEFECTS finishes the stage (100%, `acceptedOn`); the instalment after it becomes due (`get_project_contract`). HIDDEN_WORKS protocols (e.g. reinforcement before concreting) are signed the same way and finish no stage.
6. Punch list: `list_punch_items` (views OPEN, TO_VERIFY, OVERDUE, MINE, CLOSED), `update_punch_item`, `change_punch_item_status` (MARK_FIXED, then VERIFY; REOPEN; REJECT with a reason).
7. Final acceptance: `create_acceptance_protocol` kind FINAL moves the project to HANDOVER. Sign with ACCEPTED or ACCEPTED_WITH_DEFECTS; the final-handover instalment becomes due. Record how the investor confirmed a signed protocol (paper or scan) with `confirm_acceptance_protocol_by_investor`, also later.
8. Completion: `get_project_realization.completion` lists blockers; `complete_project_realization` (`realization.write`, confirm=true; `confirmOpenPunchItems=true` only if the user agrees to finish despite open defects) moves the project to CLOSED. Only a person with `realization.delete` can undo it in the app.
9. Warranty: `get_project_warranty` shows final acceptance date, contract warranty and statutory warranty (rękojmia, 5 years for building defects by default — information, not legal advice) with days left, retention, warnings, nearest deadline and a job summary (duration, contract end vs completion, price with annexes vs payments). `update_project_warranty` changes periods and retention; `record_warranty_retention_return` marks it returned. Post-completion defect: `create_warranty_defect` (report date, reporter; `warrantyCoverage` WARRANTY, STATUTORY or NONE after both periods), changed via `update_warranty_defect`, moved via `change_punch_item_status`; listed by `list_punch_items(kind=WARRANTY)`.

### Renovation without a drawing

Renovation or pricing without a drawing = project with `workType=RENOVATION` (`create_project`, or `update_project` for an existing one). MODEL is optional, so the estimate follows the address: manual positions and hand-entered quantities, also formulas such as `2*3,5*2,7` computed by the backend. Everything after the offer (contract, start with stages from estimate sections, log, acceptance, completion) matches a new construction.

### Private investor accounts

A private investor (account type INVESTOR, a PERSONAL workspace) can build or renovate their own house. PERSONAL and ORGANIZATION client accounts share plan rules, but PERSONAL starts with Offers, Customers, Invoices, Tasks, Time tracking and History off (ORGANIZATION: History off); the owner enables them in Subscription → Features. Investor portal tools depend on the account kind: use the returned entitlement, enabled modules and consented scopes, not the account label. KSeF connection management stays in the app.

The construction budget needs BUDGET included and enabled by the owner (new accounts: Pro or Advanced; legacy entitlements may differ). Tools: `get_project_budget` (limit, stages, costs, paid and to pay, notices), `add_budget_expense` (cost in a stage or shared costs, optionally with the first payment; omit the amount while the final price is unknown), `record_budget_payment` (another payment or a refund). Amounts: gross minor units (grosze). New UUID `requestId` per intended write, reused only to retry the same data. The budget itself is created and planned in the app.

## Domain Routing

Prefer the installed focused skill and select the smallest relevant topic from its reference index before domain writes. Full guide URIs below are compatibility fallbacks when the applicable skill is unavailable or guidance conflicts. Live tool schemas, catalogs and backend results remain authoritative; a guide does not authorize a write.

- Projects and BIM: projects, buildings, floors, rooms, walls, openings, slabs, roofs and measurements. Use `kaldrix-bim-authoring` and its relevant modeling topic; `references/house-building-workflow.md` indexes plan authoring. Without the skill, request `kaldrix://projects/describe-bim-model` on demand. Detailed geometry is available in the same catalog.
- Installation points, circuits, risers, underfloor heating and ventilation: use `kaldrix-bim-authoring` and the relevant topic indexed by `references/installation-authoring.md` or `references/structures-and-installations.md`. Without the skill, request `kaldrix://projects/installation-authoring` or `kaldrix://projects/structures-and-installations` on demand. Installation points need the INSTALLATIONS company module; circuits, risers, underfloor heating, ventilation, `plan_floor_installations` and circuit optimisation need INSTALLATION_DESIGN (Advanced plan, which also needs INSTALLATIONS). Circuit routes are read with `list_installation_circuits` / `get_installation_circuit_layout`.
- Structures (beams, posts, suspended ceilings, timber joists, roof framing, build-ups): use the relevant `kaldrix-bim-authoring` topic indexed by `references/structures-and-installations.md`; members and frames need STRUCTURES.
- Estimation: company materials, labour, equipment, services (recipes), price lists and project estimates. Use `kaldrix-service-estimation` and select the relevant topic indexed by `references/service-flow.md`. Without the skill, request `kaldrix://estimation/service-flow` on demand.
- Documents: project and company files, upload flows and download access. Read `kaldrix://documents/agent-documentation-policy` before storing an agent-created deliverable.
- Office : customers, KSeF and manual invoices (`list_invoices` with customer, project and payment filters; the assignment inbox `list_invoices_to_assign` → `assign_invoice`; payments `record_invoice_payment` with `confirm=true`; due dates), the project settlement `get_project_settlement`, purchase lists, tasks, construction log, project history and the company map. Invoice tools require INVOICES and their declared `invoices.*` scopes on either a PERSONAL or ORGANIZATION client account; KSeF connection management stays in the app. Project settlement tools (`get_project_settlement`, `get_project_stage_settlement`, `get_project_invoicing`, `confirm_invoice_match`) require their module, the `settlements.*` scopes and a project visible to the person.
- Contacts / Customers: `create_customer` creates a sales invoice recipient in the current tenant. It does not create a login account or a wholesaler workspace. A customer can have at most 100 projects. `lookup_company_by_nip` requires `customers.write` even though the lookup does not save data.
- Invoice vendors are derived from purchase-invoice seller tax IDs. MCP does not provide a public wholesaler catalog or a separate supplier-address-book write tool.

`confirm_invoice_match` changes saved settlement links. Read the proposed match, ask the user, then set `confirm=true`; both `settlements.write` and `settlements.delete` are required. This tool does not send an invoice to KSeF or make a payment.

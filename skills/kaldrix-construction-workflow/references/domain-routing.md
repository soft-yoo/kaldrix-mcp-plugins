## Domain Routing

Before domain writes, prefer the installed focused skill and its smallest relevant topic. Full guide URIs are fallbacks when the skill is unavailable or guidance conflicts. Live schemas, catalogs and backend results stay authoritative; a guide does not authorize a write.

- Projects and BIM (projects, buildings, floors, rooms, walls, openings, slabs, roofs, measurements): the relevant `kaldrix-bim-authoring` topic; `references/house-building-workflow.md` indexes plan authoring. Without the skill: `kaldrix://projects/describe-bim-model` on demand. Detailed geometry is in the same catalog.
- Installation points, circuits, risers, underfloor heating, ventilation: the `kaldrix-bim-authoring` topic indexed by `references/installation-authoring.md` or `references/structures-and-installations.md`; without the skill `kaldrix://projects/installation-authoring` or `kaldrix://projects/structures-and-installations` on demand. Points need the INSTALLATIONS module; circuits, risers, underfloor heating, ventilation, `plan_floor_installations` and circuit optimisation need INSTALLATION_DESIGN (Advanced plan, also needs INSTALLATIONS). Read routes with `list_installation_circuits` / `get_installation_circuit_layout`.
- Structures (beams, posts, suspended ceilings, timber joists, roof framing, build-ups): the `kaldrix-bim-authoring` topic indexed by `references/structures-and-installations.md`; members and frames need STRUCTURES.
- Estimation (company materials, labour, equipment, services/recipes, price lists, project estimates): the `kaldrix-service-estimation` topic indexed by `references/service-flow.md`; without the skill `kaldrix://estimation/service-flow` on demand.
- Documents (project and company files, upload flows, download access): read `kaldrix://documents/agent-documentation-policy` before storing an agent-created deliverable.
- Office: customers, KSeF and manual invoices (`list_invoices` with customer, project and payment filters; inbox `list_invoices_to_assign` → `assign_invoice`; payments `record_invoice_payment` with `confirm=true`; due dates), project settlement `get_project_settlement`, purchase lists, tasks, construction log, project history, company map. Invoice tools need INVOICES and their declared `invoices.*` scopes on a PERSONAL or ORGANIZATION client account; KSeF connection management stays in the app. Settlement tools (`get_project_settlement`, `get_project_stage_settlement`, `get_project_invoicing`, `confirm_invoice_match`) need their module, `settlements.*` scopes and a project visible to the person.
- Contacts / Customers: `create_customer` creates a sales invoice recipient in the current tenant — no login account, no wholesaler workspace. A customer can have at most 100 projects. `lookup_company_by_nip` needs `customers.write` although it saves nothing.

`confirm_invoice_match` changes saved settlement links: read the proposed match, ask the user, then set `confirm=true`; it needs `settlements.write` and `settlements.delete`. It does not send an invoice to KSeF or make a payment.
